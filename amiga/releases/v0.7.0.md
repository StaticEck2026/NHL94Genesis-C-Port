The native Amiga 1200 (AGA) port of NHL '94, second playable preview: much faster drawing, the game timed
at the cartridge's own rate, and a host menu before the title.

**Asset:** `nhl94-aga-v0.7.0.adf`, SHA-256 `79c2ccbcf9b709e12d09963f7c4b3b4320682d5945e598ba96aa34d38d8b3195` (built from commit ab314d0).

## What you need

* An Amiga with AGA and a 68020 or better; the target is an **A1200 with Fast RAM** (D-14).
* Kickstart 3.0 (39.106) or 3.1 (40.068).
* **Your own dump of the 1 MB US/Europe NHL '94 cartridge.** The disk holds none of the cartridge's art,
  sound or data: everything is made from your cartridge on your Amiga. Put it at `NHL94ROM:nhl94.bin`,
  `RAM:nhl94.bin` or beside the program, or on two floppies (`NHL94ROMA:nhl94.rom.1` and
  `NHL94ROMB:nhl94.rom.2`, the halves). The program checks its CRC32.

## Playing

Boot the disk. The host menu comes first: 60 Hz or 50 Hz, the joystick ports, the keyboard's player, the
4 Way Play adapter, the audio filter; then **Start the game**. Keyboard: cursor keys, Z = A, X = B,
C (or Space, Return) = C, P = Start, Esc quits. Joystick: fire 1 = B, fire 2 = A. CD32 pad: red B, blue C,
yellow and green A, play Start.

## Status (from a cycle-exact emulated A1200; real hardware confirmation comes before 1.0.0)

* Every picture is drawn exactly as the reference (checked on every pass of the test scripts).
* The game runs at the cartridge's own speed (59.923 passes a second, 49.702 on 50 Hz), timed by a CIA
  timer. Where a picture cannot be drawn in time, that picture is left out and the game keeps its pace:
  about one match pass in eight, the title while its credits scroll, the in-match roster screen while its
  background moves. Drawing every picture in time is the next work.
* The sound is as in 0.6.0 (mono, fixed instrument timbres).

The full log of the work is `amiga/STATUS.md`, the changes `amiga/CHANGELOG.md`, the test sheet
`amiga/tests/v0.7.0.md`.

## About the cartridge data in the program

The game's compiled C holds some short tables and strings from the cartridge, all from source committed in
this repository, as the host releases of this project do (allowed by the owner, D-12). The disk check
(A-LEGAL) passes: 625 such windows, all inside the program, and no other byte of the ROM on the disk.
